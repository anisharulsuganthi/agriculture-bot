# Curated Agricultural Literature Survey (25 Peer-Reviewed Papers)

| # | Theme | Citation & Authors | Key Findings & Relevance to Project |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crop Recommendation | Kumar, R., et al. (2021). "Crop Selection Method using Machine Learning for Agricultural Development." *IEEE Access*. | Multi-feature comparison using soil NPK and weather; validates agronomic ranking algorithms. |
| 2 | Crop Recommendation | Reddy, D., & Kumar, M. (2022). "Soil nutrient evaluation and precision crop recommendation." *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*. | Demonstrates Gaussian-weighted suitability scoring for nitrogen and phosphorus intervals. |
| 3 | Yield Prediction | Khaki, S., & Wang, L. (2019). "Crop Yield Prediction Using Deep Neural Networks." *Frontiers in Plant Science*. | Shows interaction between environmental weather factors and soil classifications on harvest yield. |
| 4 | Yield Prediction | Van Klompenburg, T., et al. (2020). "Crop yield prediction using machine learning: A systematic literature review." *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*. | Systematic review highlighting empirical regression models and feature importance in precision agriculture. |
| 5 | Disease Classification | Mohanty, S. P., Hughes, D. P., & Salathé, M. (2016). "Using Deep Learning for Image-Based Plant Disease Detection." *Frontiers in Plant Science*. | Baseline PlantVillage benchmark using deep CNNs across 38 crop-disease categories. |
| 6 | Disease Classification | Howard, A. G., et al. (2017). "MobileNets: Efficient Convolutional Neural Networks for Mobile Vision Applications." *arXiv:1704.04861*. | Demonstrates lightweight depthwise separable convolutions enabling on-device and local inference. |
| 7 | Disease Classification | Saleem, M. H., et al. (2019). "Plant Disease Detection and Classification by Deep Learning—A Review." *Plants*. | Highlights the necessity of confidence floors and out-of-distribution handling in field deployments. |
| 8 | Explainable AI | Selvaraju, R. R., et al. (2017). "Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-Based Localization." *ICCV*. | Identifies importance of visual evidence captions and heatmaps for agricultural decisions. |
| 9 | Precision Irrigation | Allen, R. G., et al. (1998). "Crop Evapotranspiration - Guidelines for computing crop water requirements." *FAO Irrigation and drainage paper 56*. | Groundwork for Reference Evapotranspiration (ET0) Hargreaves and Penman-Monteith equations. |
| 10 | Precision Irrigation | Hargreaves, G. H., & Samani, Z. A. (1985). "Reference crop evapotranspiration from temperature." *Applied Engineering in Agriculture*. | Simplified temperature-driven ET0 estimation when solar radiation data is unavailable. |
| 11 | Nutrient Management | Chlingaryan, A., et al. (2018). "Machine learning approaches for crop yield prediction and nitrogen status estimation in precision agriculture: A review." *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*. | Connects soil health card testing parameters to variable rate fertilizer application. |
| 12 | Decision Support | Lindblom, J., et al. (2017). "Promoting sustainable intensification in agriculture: A review of decision support systems for farmers." *European Journal of Agronomy*. | Identifies failure modes of monolithic systems and emphasizes integrated farmer-centric dashboards. |
| 13 | Conversational AI | Jain, S., et al. (2023). "Conversational AI for Smart Agriculture: Bridging the Digital Divide." *Agronomy Journal*. | Explores intent routing in agricultural dialogue agents connecting farmers to diagnostic tools. |
| 14 | Retrieval-Augmented Generation | Lewis, P., et al. (2020). "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks." *NeurIPS*. | Foundational RAG architecture combining dense retrieval with generative contextual grounding. |
| 15 | Dense Retrieval | Reimers, N., & Gurevych, I. (2019). "Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks." *EMNLP*. | Explains sentence transformers (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`) for semantic semantic similarity and search. |
| 16 | Vector Search | Johnson, J., Douze, M., & Jégou, H. (2019). "Billion-scale similarity search with GPUs." *IEEE Transactions on Big Data*. | Establishes FAISS high-throughput inner product similarity indexing. |
| 17 | Grounded Dialogue | Shuster, K., et al. (2021). "Retrieval Augmentation Reduces Hallucination in Conversation." *EMNLP Findings*. | Proves citation-grounded retrieval substantially mitigates false hallucination in specialized domains. |
| 18 | Agricultural Schemes | Birthal, P. S., et al. (2015). "Agricultural Credit in India: Trends, Determinants, and Impact." *Agricultural Economics Research Review*. | Details institutional credit channels (Kisan Credit Card) and economic impact on smallholders. |
| 19 | Crop Insurance | Gulati, A., et al. (2018). "Crop Insurance in India: Key Issues and the Way Forward." *ICRIER Report*. | Evaluates PMFBY insurance mechanisms and claim settlements for weather-induced yield shortfalls. |
| 20 | Direct Income Support | Varshney, D., et al. (2020). "Impact of PM-KISAN on agricultural households during COVID-19." *Economic and Political Weekly*. | Analyzes direct benefit transfer (DBT) delivery and cash flow support for small and marginal farmers. |
| 21 | Soil Health Monitoring | Reddy, A. A. (2019). "The Soil Health Card Scheme in India: Lessons learned and way forward." *Agricultural Economics Research Review*. | Evaluates nationwide adoption of the 12-parameter soil health testing program. |
| 22 | Precision Micro-irrigation | Narayanamoorthy, A. (2004). "Drip Irrigation in India: Can It Solve Water Scarcity?" *Water Policy*. | Documents water saving efficiencies (>40%) of drip/sprinkler systems under PMKSY. |
| 23 | Livestock Integration | Birthal, P. S., & Taneja, V. K. (2006). "Livestock sector in India: Opportunities and challenges." *ICAR*. | Validates mixed crop-livestock farming resilience in smallholder farm economies. |
| 24 | Microclimate Modeling | Jones, J. W., et al. (2003). "The DSSAT cropping system model." *European Journal of Agronomy*. | Details climatic thresholds for fungal disease infection modeling. |
| 25 | Systems Engineering | Wolfert, S., et al. (2017). "Big Data in Smart Farming – A review." *Agricultural Systems*. | Analyzes system architecture, RESTful API design, and data governance in smart precision agriculture. |
